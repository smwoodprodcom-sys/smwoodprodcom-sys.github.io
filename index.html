Rclone Local File Access

This page describes a locally operated Rclone OAuth client used by its owner to access Google Drive through the Rclone command-line application and local automation.

Operator-configured AI agents may send selected Drive content to external model/API providers. See the privacy notice. Those transfers depend on the selected agent and task; they are not performed by this static site or by mounting Drive alone.

Permissions: The configured Google Drive scope allows broad access to the authorizing account's Drive. Depending on the local Rclone commands and automation chosen by the operator, files may be listed, read, created, updated, moved, renamed, or deleted. Authorizing the app does not itself make Drive files public.

Each Google account must separately authorize access. A person should authorize this client only if they intend to use their own Rclone setup.
