This OAuth client supports local Rclone use. It is not a hosted storage, synchronization, or file-sharing service. The static website only provides information and does not operate on Google Drive files.

Authorization and permitted use

Only authorize access for a Google account you control or are expressly permitted to administer. The OAuth scope is broad and can allow Rclone commands to read, create, change, move, or delete Drive files. Use commands and automation only when you understand their effects and have authority over the affected files.

Your responsibilities

  • Keep OAuth tokens and local configuration files private and secure.
  • Maintain independent backups of important files. Rclone commands can modify or delete remote content.
  • Review scripts and automation before allowing them to operate on Drive.
  • When configured agents send Drive-derived content to external AI/model/API services, check the specific recipient, data-use settings, and applicable authority/consent first. Google Drive OAuth authorization alone is not permission for every external provider to receive file content.
  • Follow Google's terms and policies, Rclone's license and documentation, and applicable laws.
